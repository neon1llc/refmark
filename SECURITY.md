# Reporting a problem

Refmark fetches and parses pages from the internet. The problems that matter most are ones where a hostile page makes it run code, write files outside its cache, or exhaust memory.

- **A security problem:** email security@neon1.tech. Don't open a public issue.
- **A wrong result** (a change missed, or one reported that isn't): open an issue with the URL, the scope, and the output of `refmark show <id>`.

I reply within a few days and credit you in the changelog if you want.
